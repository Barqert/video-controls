How to write shortcuts

    
A subset of mpv’s input.conf format is supported.
      

      Supported commands: seek, set, add, multiply,
       cycle, cycle-values, vf toggle hflip/vflip, ignore.
      

      Supported properties: video-pan-x/y, video-zoom,
      video-rotate, speed, volume, audio-delay (positive only),
      pause, mute.
    

    
Shortcuts briefly show the new value at the video's top-left corner. To keep a command quiet,
      add no-osd before it, for example UP no-osd add volume 5.

    
Speeds above 16× play at 16× and skip forward in small steps to make up the difference.
      This also works with multiply-decay. The displayed speed is the target:
      playback will be jumpy, and buffering can make it slower. Skipping stops while paused or buffering.
      Videos that cannot seek stay at 16×.


    
Extra commands added by this extension

    

      
CommandWhat it does

      
        
multiply-decay PROPERTY X YMultiplies PROPERTY by X and then decays back to the original values, with Y being the decay half-life in seconds

        
reset-allUndo moves, zoom, rotation, and flips. This also ends their temporary effects. Speed, sound, and pause stay as they are.
